Yifan Bai

I am a PhD student at the School of Mathematical Sciences, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, advised by Jingwei Liang, and currently a visiting PhD student at the Mathematics Institute, University of Warwick, supervised by Clarice Poon.我是上海交通大学数学科学学院的博士生，导师为 Jingwei Liang；目前在华威大学数学研究院担任访问博士生，导师为 Clarice Poon。

My research focuses on dimension reduction methods for large-scale optimization problems and AI4MATH (Lean-based autoformalization).我的研究方向是大规模优化问题中的降维方法，以及 AI4MATH（基于 Lean 的自动形式化）。

I completed my undergraduate studies at the School of Mathematics, Sun Yat-sen University, majoring in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics.我本科毕业于中山大学数学学院，专业为数学与应用数学。
